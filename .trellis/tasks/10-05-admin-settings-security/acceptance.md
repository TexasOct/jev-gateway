## Reproduced product defect

R1, GS3 / IA4 / IA5 / S08: a validation started before authentication suspension can issue a configuration PUT after reconnection.

Reproduction uses the production App and a closed loopback fixture:

1. Open Settings, choose `fixture/existing` in `#settings-default-model`, and save. Hold the validation response with a deterministic promise barrier.
2. Select the Green theme preset. Return 401 for its independent theme PUT, causing the App to suspend the workspace and show Connect.
3. Reconnect through the real login form and wait until the connection page closes.
4. Release the earlier validation response. Wait until the default-model region reports `aria-busy=false`.
5. Observe one provider-configuration PUT. Expected: zero PUTs from the operation started under the earlier admission, with the draft available for explicit retry.
